<h1>Dashboard</h1>

<p>
Live state from the owning host, captured by <code>scripts/site-state-fetch.sh</code>
(<code>make state</code>) and substituted at build time.
A key with no value renders as <code>unavailable</code>.
</p>

<table>
  <tr><th>Key</th><th>Value</th></tr>
  <tr><td>Version</td><td>__VERSION__</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Fetched</td><td>__FETCHED__</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Host</td><td>__HOST__</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Branch</td><td>__BRANCH__</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Commit</td><td>__COMMIT__</td></tr>
</table>

<h2>This project</h2>

<p>
The engine is <code>sources/ruleset-run.mjs</code>, the renderer is <code>sources/ruleset-render.mjs</code>,
and the command-line tool is <code>sources/ruleset-command.mjs</code>.
The round-trip example is generated from <code>dataflow.in/round-trip.json</code>.
</p>
